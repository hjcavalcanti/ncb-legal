NCB Publicador — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. What this app is

NCB Publicador is a private desktop tool operated by NCB, a Brazilian news channel, to upload NCB's own edited video clips and captions to NCB's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

2. Who may use it

Only authorized NCB staff may use the tool, and only with the TikTok account that NCB controls. Each post is created by NCB from content NCB has the right to publish.

3. Content and compliance

NCB is solely responsible for the videos and captions it publishes. NCB uses the tool in accordance with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service, the Content Posting API guidelines and the TikTok Community Guidelines. Clips keep the original speech unaltered, attribute statements to the people who made them and identify their sources.

4. No warranty

The tool is provided "as is". Availability depends on TikTok's services. NCB may change or stop using the tool at any time.
